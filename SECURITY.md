## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of **AetherGuard**, autonomous AI agent financial boundaries, and formal invariant kernels extremely seriously.

If you believe you have discovered a vulnerability, invariant bypass, or hardware enclave isolation issue, please follow responsible disclosure:

1. **Do NOT open a public GitHub issue.**
2. Send an email to **`guptakumarranjeet150@gmail.com`** with:
   - Detailed description of the invariant failure or boundary condition.
   - Proof-of-concept test case or reproduction script.
   - Potential impact on agent treasury balances or contract execution.
3. You will receive an acknowledgment within **24 hours**, and patch validation will be prioritized immediately.

## Bug Bounty & Recognition

Valid, high-impact security vulnerability discoveries will be publicly acknowledged in our Hall of Fame and rewarded according to severity.
